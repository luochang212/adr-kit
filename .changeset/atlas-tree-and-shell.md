The decision atlas reader now renders each record's `## Deliberation` appendix as a nested tree — questions, options, states, recommendation, override, and reasons — parsed by the same core the other tree renderers use. The three product terms are unified as decision atlas, decision map, and deliberation tree across the CLI, docs, skills, and site copy.
